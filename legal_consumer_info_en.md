# Legal & Consumer Information

<p style="color: #666; font-size: 0.9em;">Last updated: 2 August 2026</p>

---

## Who provides this app

Sabiowl is published by YukimiWorks LLC (Sabiowl Project).

- Email: support@sabiowl.com

Email is the fastest way to reach us. If you need our registered postal address or
telephone number, ask at the address above and we will provide it.

---

## What you can buy

Sabiowl is free to download and use. The app offers optional one-time in-app
purchases of Diamonds, an in-app currency used for cosmetic and convenience items.
Purchases are never required to track habits or to keep your progress.

Diamonds can also be earned inside the app without paying, by completing habits and
other activities.

In-app purchases are currently available on iOS only.

---

## Prices

Prices are shown in your local currency on the purchase screen before you confirm.
The price you see there is the price Apple charges you, and it includes any taxes
Apple applies in your region.

We may change prices. A change applies only to purchases made after it takes effect.

---

## When you receive what you bought

Diamonds are added to your account as soon as the store confirms your purchase.
Delivery may take a few seconds depending on your connection.

---

## Refunds

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple under its own policies, not by us.
Please use Apple's refund process:

- [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)

If a purchase was charged but the Diamonds did not arrive, contact us at
support@sabiowl.com and we will look into it.

---

## Cancelling

These are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. There is nothing to cancel.

---

## Requirements

An internet connection is required. Some features need an account, which you can
create by signing in with Google or Apple.
