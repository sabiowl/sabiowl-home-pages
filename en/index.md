# Sabiowl

**Sabiowl** is a mobile app for iOS and Android that helps you keep up your daily
habits, tasks and plans by making them feel like a game. Every time you record a
habit you earn experience, and your character levels up — an RPG-style sense of
growth that makes it easier to keep going.

This is the official site for Sabiowl. It collects the app's features, legal
documents, contact details and release notes.

## What Sabiowl does

- **Records and shows your habits** — log what you do each day and see how it
  accumulates on a timeline.
- **Growth, RPG style** — completing habits earns experience. Your character
  levels up and six stats grow with you: Fitness, Learning, Health, Spirit,
  Creativity and Contribution.
- **Google Calendar integration** — bring your calendar events in and see them
  alongside habits and to-dos on a single timeline. (Import only; stored on your
  device.)
- **Characters and gacha** — choose from several characters, with a light layer
  of game design to keep motivation up.
- **Battles** — put the stats you have built through your habits to work against
  enemies.
