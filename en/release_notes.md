# Sabiowl Release Notes

<p style="color: #666; font-size: 0.9em;">Last updated: 9 August 2026</p>

---

What changed in each version of Sabiowl, newest first.
What we are preparing next is at the end.

---

## v1.0.5 (latest)

Adjustments so that less of what you build up goes to waste.

### 📝 Search in Quick Notes

- A search field at the top of the notes screen narrows down what you have written
- Matches are highlighted in yellow

### 🎟 Much higher ticket limits

- Daily: 5 → **30**
- Weekly: 4 → **10**

This came from hearing that tickets could quietly disappear when you went a few
days without drawing. There is now enough room that a month away costs you
nothing 🪶

### 🗓 Calendar improvements

- Events imported from Google Calendar now appear on the calendar screen's
  timeline as well
- You can turn off automatic creation of default events all at once

### ✨ Other

- New entries now default to the "Other" category, which spreads experience
  evenly across all six stats
- Fixed a case where challenge rewards could sit unclaimed

---

## v1.0.4 (released 29 July 2026)

### 📝 Quick Notes

Somewhere to write down what you have thought of but not yet decided what to
make of. Sorting it into an event, a to-do or a habit can wait.

- Voice input, so you can write by speaking. The transcription is done by your
  device's operating system; the audio itself is never sent to our servers
- Deleted notes go to the trash, so you can restore them afterwards

You can turn the feature on and off under **Settings → Quick Notes**.

## v1.0.3 (released 25 July 2026)

### 🌙 More life in the sleeping world

- Clouds drifting over the castle town at midday
- A new campfire for the forest camp at night
- The fragment pop-up now draws a cut-out of the scene itself

### 🎁 Gifts to friends

- What used to be an XP boost alone is now a **set of three: an XP boost, coins
  and a battle ticket**

### ✨ Better to use

- The bottom menu now slides between screens in the direction you are moving
- A separate screen for connection and server problems, so they are no longer
  confused with maintenance
- You can change your name and your character from the stats screen
- Fixed weapons won from the gacha not appearing in your shop inventory

## v1.0.2 (released July 2026)

### 🌙 The sleeping world

A place where what you build up day by day takes shape as a scene.

- Your first completed habit of the day creates one outline fragment (grey)
- Winning a battle that day turns it into a colour fragment
- When every fragment has colour, the scene is complete and starts to move

### 📖 User Guide

- You can now read how each screen works from inside the app (at the top of the
  ☰ menu)

### ✨ Other

- Reorganised the ☰ menu so the screens you use most are two taps away
- Titles you have entered once are remembered on your device and offered as
  suggestions next time

## v1.0.1 (released July 2026)

A follow-up to the initial release. This version adds one-time in-app purchases,
so you can top up diamonds when you need to.

### 💎 Diamond packs (iOS)

- **¥120**: 120 diamonds
- **¥600**: 660 diamonds (10% more)
- **¥1,200**: 1,440 diamonds (20% more)

Payment goes through the standard App Store flow.

For the full terms of sale, see
[Legal & Consumer Information](/legal_consumer_info_en.html).

### 🍃 Rest days and rest fruit discontinued

There was no screen for setting a rest day, and the fruit accumulated with no
visible use, so we removed both. Protecting a day off is now handled solely by
the **streak protection stone** (💎 30 each in the shop). Turn on
**Settings → Streak Protection** and one is spent automatically the moment a
streak would break 🪶

## v1.0.0 (released late June 2026)

The first public release. This version pairs the core loop of keeping a habit
going with RPG-style growth.

### ✨ Main features

#### 🌱 Habits and to-dos
- Habits (count-based and checklist-based) and to-dos, managed on one screen
- 11 categories mapped across 6 stats: Fitness, Learning, Health, Spirit,
  Creativity and Contribution
- Streaks and rest days, so you can keep going without forcing it

#### ⭐ RPG-style growth
- Completing a habit earns experience and stat points
- Levelling up gives you points to spend, and you decide which of the six stats
  to grow
- Reaching a streak milestone earns diamonds

#### 🎴 Characters and gacha
- 14 characters and 14 jobs (characters are released in stages, roughly one or
  two a month)
- Three gacha types: daily, weekly, and monthly (guaranteed SSR)
- Gacha tickets come from completing habits. The guaranteed SSR ticket is
  granted automatically once you have completed 21 days in a month

#### ⚔️ Guild (battles)
- An ATB battle system, with 24 enemies to choose from
- Your weapon, job modifiers and character HP combine into a real choice about
  how to fight
- Winning earns coins, experience and diamonds

#### 👥 Friends
- Add friends with an 8-digit friend ID
- Send and receive requests, and view a friend's profile
- Send one XP boost (1.5× for 15 minutes) to a friend each day

#### 📅 Timeline
- Lay out a day from a template
- Google Calendar integration (import)
- Completed events earn experience and coins

#### 🦉 Sabi (the mascot)
- Sabi, a gentlemanly owl, watches over your habits quietly
- He speaks up in the morning, at night, when you keep something going, and when
  things stall

#### 🏅 Achievements
- 30 badges that make your accumulated effort visible
- Earning a badge earns diamonds

#### 💎 New user bonus
- Day 1: 500 diamonds, 3 daily tickets, 3 weekly tickets
- Days 2–7: 100 diamonds a day
- Day 8 onward: 20 diamonds a day

All of these arrive **the moment you complete your first task of the day**. They
are a reward for taking a step, not simply for opening the app 🪶

### ⚠️ Known limitations
- In-app purchases (diamond packs) arrive in v1.0.1
- The job mastery system (switching jobs across characters) is planned for v1.1
  and later

## What comes next (v1.1 and later)

We are preparing the following while we watch how the app is used after launch.
**The contents and timing may change**, so please read this as a rough guide.

### 🎴 More characters
- **Released in stages**, roughly one or two a month, from the characters not
  yet available
- We will announce the timing in the app beforehand

### ⚔️ Job mastery
- Moving on from the v1.0 design where each character is tied to one job, toward
  switching and mastering jobs across characters
- Skills you have built up in a job should carry over to other characters

### 💎 Crystal inventory
- Equip the crystals you have collected to draw out their effects
- Six kinds, one per category: Fitness, Learning, Health, Spirit, Creativity and
  Contribution

### ✨ "NEW" banner on the gacha screen
- Makes newly added characters easier to spot
- Tap to go straight to the character's page

### 🌍 A world you can explore
- More NPCs with distinct roles, such as a weapon shop and a gacha stand, added
  in stages
- Growing from "a habit app with rewards" into "a habit app with a world you can
  explore"

### 💎 More purchase options (under consideration)
- A monthly subscription
- Details and pricing will follow as we see how monthly active use develops
